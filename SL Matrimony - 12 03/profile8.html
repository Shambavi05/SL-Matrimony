Raju Porutham

Rajju porutham is considered a vital porutham to be assessed as it checks the longevity of a husband in a marriage. A long-lived husband may bring overall happiness to the wife. It is an important factor to be considered while fixing a marriage. Rajju depicts the mangalya bala, or strength, in a marriage. If both the boy and the girl belong to the same birth star, then Rajju dosham occurs and is not considered good for the longevity of the husband.
